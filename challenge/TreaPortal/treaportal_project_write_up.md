# TreaPortal: Comprehensive Business & Workforce Management System

TreaPortal is a centralized organizational management platform designed to streamline administrative operations, task delegation, and workforce analytics. Aligning with the features outlined in the `TreaPortal.md` reference document, the system provides management teams with a comprehensive suite of tools to monitor business health and coordinate daily activities through an intuitive, data-driven interface.

---

## Executive Dashboard & Analytics

The platform features a dynamic dashboard that delivers real-time financial insights, including current month revenue and historical revenue trends via interactive charts. It also highlights top-level organizational metrics such as total tasks, active members, and operational teams alongside a quick view of recent tasks.

![Dashboard Overview](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/08958aa7-a40d-4dca-8710-5cd87cb32857)

---

## Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

TreaPortal includes a robust security and permissions module. Administrators can easily define, edit, and assign specific user roles—such as Access Control, Employee, Inventory Manager, and Manager. This ensures that personnel only access the data and modules relevant to their direct responsibilities.

![Role Management](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/29ed5e3b-bef0-49c5-940d-aa1fab709a64)

---

## Employee Administration & Profiling

The system maintains a complete employee directory detailing names, organizational positions, contact information, and onboarding dates for a quick administrative overview.

![Employee Directory](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d654d491-396a-4a54-9a78-4ce5d5dc8866)

Furthermore, individual employee profile cards allow managers to deep-dive into specific staff members. From here, management can track daily reports, view assigned tasks, and access personal details in one consolidated, user-friendly view.

![Employee Profile](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/79cf0a30-4abe-4814-9846-247dbbb41c63)

---

## Advanced Task Management

Users can seamlessly deploy assignments using the "Add Task" module. This feature supports comprehensive task detailing, including title descriptions, document attachments, team assignments, priority levels (e.g., High, Medium, Low), and strict scheduling deadlines.

![Task Deployment Modal](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ef4e49e5-9fb2-41b3-9053-17337d52b54f)

---

## Calendar & Milestone Tracking

An integrated calendar view allows teams to visually track project timelines, task deadlines, and key deliverables across the month. This visual schedule ensures that workforce efforts remain aligned with organizational goals and that no deadlines are missed.

![Calendar View](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/381e53d9-2794-4606-8b9a-65c0991d5fc1)
